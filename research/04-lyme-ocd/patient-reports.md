# Topic 4: Lyme and OCD — Patient Reports

## Methodology and citation-integrity note

Patterns synthesized from r/Lyme, r/lymedisease, r/OCD, Phoenix Rising, and
PANS/PANDAS parent communities. Forum content is **paraphrased, never quoted,
never attributed to a user**; excerpts link to **platform-level URLs** with
**approximate eras**. Mental-health content is described non-clinically; the
project disclaimer's crisis resources apply.

## Recurring Symptom Patterns

### Pattern: New or worsened intrusive thoughts/compulsions during Lyme illness
- **Description:** Posters with Lyme describe onset or marked worsening of
  intrusive thoughts, contamination fears, checking, or "brain-lock" alongside
  cognitive fog, sometimes resolving as they feel they recover.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, r/lymedisease.
- **Frequency:** occasional-to-common within Lyme communities.
- **Relationship to literature:** consistent with neuropsychiatric-Lyme reports
  [T4-S001][T4-S004]; uncontrolled.
- **Source IDs:** [T4-F001].

### Pattern: Abrupt-onset OCD in children with infection ("PANS-like")
- **Description:** Parents describe sudden, dramatic OCD/tic onset after an
  infection (strep, sometimes Lyme/tick exposure), with restricted eating,
  separation anxiety, and rage — matching PANS/PANDAS descriptions.
- **Platforms:** PANS/PANDAS parent forums, r/Lyme.
- **Frequency:** common within PANS communities; Lyme-attributed subset smaller.
- **Relationship to literature:** matches PANDAS/PANS phenotype [T4-S005];
  Lyme-trigger attribution is [T4-S004][T4-S008] (speculative).
- **Source IDs:** [T4-F002].

### Pattern: Improvement of OCD symptoms attributed to antibiotics
- **Description:** Some posters report OCD symptoms easing on antibiotics for
  Lyme; others report no change or worsening anxiety. Mixed, uncontrolled, often
  co-occurring with SSRIs/therapy.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, PANS forums.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** echoes case reports [T4-S008]; confounded;
  contradicts mainstream caution about antibiotics for OCD [T4-S007].
- **Source IDs:** [T4-F003].

### Pattern: "Health-anxiety / contamination" loop around the diagnosis itself
- **Description:** A reflexive pattern where Lyme/Morgellons fears become the
  *content* of OCD — compulsive symptom-checking, reassurance-seeking, lab-test
  collecting — blurring whether OCD is caused by or directed at the illness.
- **Platforms:** r/OCD, r/Lyme.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** not well captured in literature; an important
  confound for causal claims. Flagged as **no firm literature linkage**.
- **Source IDs:** [T4-F004].

## What patients describe that literature handles only partially

- **Directionality is genuinely ambiguous** in lived reports: chronic illness can
  cause OCD-like checking, and OCD can latch onto illness fears — a feedback loop
  literature rarely disentangles.
- **Symptom timing relative to treatment** is the data patients have and trials
  lack; it is suggestive but uncontrolled.
