# Topic 12: Lyme and Vaccines — Patient Reports

## Methodology and citation-integrity note

Patterns synthesized from r/Lyme, COVID-vaccine-injury and ME/CFS/long-COVID
communities, and chronic-illness forums. **Provenance:** every excerpt below is a
`synthesized-pattern` (pattern-level synthesis using platform-level links), **not
a quoted post or verified thread** — see the project Methodology page. Forum
content is **paraphrased, never attributed to a user**. This is a charged,
misinformation-prone space; patterns are reported descriptively, and the
disclaimer's framing (this is not medical advice) especially applies.

## Recurring Patterns

### Pattern: Lyme/chronic-illness flare reported after COVID-19 vaccination
- **Description:** Posters with prior Lyme or chronic illness describe a symptom
  flare (fatigue, pain, neuro symptoms) in the days–weeks after a COVID-19 vaccine
  dose, interpreting it as the vaccine "waking up" or worsening their Lyme.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, vaccine-injury communities.
- **Frequency:** common (within these communities).
- **Relationship to literature:** consistent with non-specific immune-activation
  flares [T12-S009] and the PVS research direction [T12-S007]; the
  *Borrelia-reactivation* interpretation is unproven; confounded by nocebo and
  natural fluctuation.
- **Source IDs:** [T12-F001].

### Pattern: Similar flare reported after COVID-19 *infection* (not just vaccine)
- **Description:** Many of the same posters report comparable or worse flares
  after COVID infection itself, sometimes acknowledging the infection hit harder
  than the shot.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, long-COVID forums.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** important confounder — post-viral syndromes and
  EBV reactivation follow infection [T12-S008][T12-S009]; undercuts a
  vaccine-specific causal story.
- **Source IDs:** [T12-F002].

### Pattern: "The vaccine gave me / reactivated my Lyme" causal claim
- **Description:** Stronger causal posts asserting the COVID vaccine *caused* or
  *reactivated* active Lyme, often shared with broader anti-vaccine content.
- **Platforms:** vaccine-injury / anti-vaccine communities, r/Lyme.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** unsupported; no mechanism; flagged `fringe`
  [T12-S011]. Reported here to document the discourse, not endorse it.
- **Source IDs:** [T12-F003].

### Pattern: LYMErix history invoked as proof vaccines harm Lyme patients
- **Description:** Posters cite the withdrawn LYMErix vaccine as evidence that
  "Lyme vaccines hurt people," extending it to distrust of COVID and future Lyme
  (VLA15) vaccines.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, LymeNet.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** cites real history [T12-S003] but misreads it —
  the trial showed no increased arthritis and withdrawal was a risk-perception
  cascade [T12-S001][T12-S004][T12-S005].
- **Source IDs:** [T12-F004].

### Pattern: Mixed / no-change and pro-vaccine reports
- **Description:** A counter-current of posters report no flare, or that they
  tolerated vaccines fine, and some actively push back on causal claims and
  misinformation within the communities.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, long-COVID forums.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** consistent with the vaccine-safety consensus
  [T12-S011]; a reminder that loud flare reports are not the whole distribution.
- **Source IDs:** [T12-F005].

## What patients describe that literature handles only partially

- **The vaccine-vs-infection attribution split** is vivid in forums: the same
  flare gets blamed on the shot or the virus depending on timing and community —
  a natural confound the causal claims rarely address.
- **Persistent "circulating spike" anxiety** (from the LISTEN preprint [T12-S007])
  is spreading in communities faster than the preliminary, unreplicated evidence
  warrants — a misinformation-velocity pattern worth noting.
