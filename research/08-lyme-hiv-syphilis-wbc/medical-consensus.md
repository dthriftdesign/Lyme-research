# Topic 8: Lyme, HIV, Syphilis, and White Blood Cell Disruption — Medical Consensus

> Scope: how each infection perturbs **leukocytes / the immune system**, and what
> the three have in common mechanistically. Builds on Topic 7. The contested
> "chronic-Lyme immune-dysfunction" biomarkers (e.g., CD57) are in
> `non-consensus.md`.

## Overview

All three pathogens are **immune-disrupting**, but in distinct, well-characterized
ways:

- **HIV** directly **depletes CD4+ T lymphocytes**, the central failure of
  adaptive immunity in AIDS [T8-S007].
- **Borrelia burgdorferi** is a master **immune evader and modulator**: it
  occupies lymph nodes, drives abnormal B-cell proliferation, generates
  **structurally defective, short-lived germinal centers**, and **suppresses
  long-lived humoral immunity** — including against unrelated antigens — leaving
  hosts vulnerable to reinfection [T8-S001][T8-S002][T8-S003][T8-S008]. Chronic
  antigenic stimulation can, in some hosts, drive **B. burgdorferi-associated
  primary cutaneous B-cell (marginal-zone) lymphoma**, which can regress with
  antibiotics — a direct infection→lymphoproliferation link [T8-S004][T8-S005].
- **Treponema pallidum (syphilis)** dysregulates innate and adaptive immunity and
  interacts bidirectionally with HIV (each worsens the other's course).

The unifying theme: **persistent spirochetes and HIV both reshape the lymphocyte
compartment to survive**, and these immune perturbations explain co-infection
synergy, diagnostic serology failures (Topic 7), and some downstream pathology.

## Established Mechanisms

### Borrelia immune evasion and humoral suppression
- **Antigenic variation** (VlsE) and complement evasion let *Borrelia* persist
  [T8-S001].
- It induces **rapid, dysregulated B-cell proliferation** in lymph nodes with
  deterioration of T- and B-cell zones [T8-S001].
- Germinal centers formed during infection are **abnormal and non-durable**,
  failing to generate robust memory B cells and long-lived plasma cells —
  **broad, lasting suppression of humoral immunity** [T8-S002][T8-S003]. This is
  a leading mainstream explanation for **reinfection susceptibility** and for why
  Lyme serology is imperfect.
- Innate/adaptive manipulation also enables persistence in reservoir hosts
  [T8-S008].

### Borrelia-driven lymphoproliferation → lymphoma
Chronic *Borrelia* antigenic drive can sustain a clonal B-cell population in
skin; **primary cutaneous marginal-zone lymphoma** associated with *Borrelia*
(notably *B. afzelii* in Europe) can **regress after antibiotic eradication** —
analogous to *H. pylori*-driven gastric MALT lymphoma [T8-S004][T8-S005]. This is
the clearest mainstream example of *Borrelia* causing a defined WBC (B-cell)
disorder.

### HIV: CD4+ T-cell depletion
HIV depletes CD4+ T cells through direct cytopathic effects, immune activation,
pyroptosis, and lymphoid-tissue destruction, collapsing adaptive immunity
[T8-S007]. This is the archetypal "WBC disruption" and the backdrop against which
co-infections (including Lyme and *Babesia*) behave atypically (Topic 7).

### Syphilis and HIV cross-talk
Syphilis increases HIV acquisition/transmission and can alter HIV immune markers;
HIV alters syphilis serology and progression — a clinically important
bidirectional immune interaction.

## Diagnostic Approach (mainstream)

- Interpret **lymphocyte counts/subsets** in context: CD4 count for HIV staging;
  recognize that acute infections cause transient leukocyte shifts.
- Consider **Borrelia-associated lymphoma** in persistent cutaneous lymphoid
  infiltrates in endemic areas; test for *Borrelia* and consider antibiotic
  trial before aggressive lymphoma therapy [T8-S004][T8-S005].
- Recognize that **Borrelia-induced humoral suppression** can blunt serologic
  responses [T8-S002].

## Treatment Consensus (mainstream)

- Treat each infection per standards (ART for HIV; stage-appropriate penicillin
  for syphilis; guideline antibiotics for Lyme).
- For *Borrelia*-associated cutaneous marginal-zone lymphoma, **antibiotics can
  induce remission** in suitable cases [T8-S005].

## Open Questions Within Mainstream

- **How durable and clinically significant is Borrelia-induced humoral
  suppression** in humans (vs. mice)? Actively studied [T8-S002][T8-S003].
- **What fraction of cutaneous B-cell lymphomas are Borrelia-driven**, and
  geographic variation (Europe vs. US species)? [T8-S004]
- **Does Borrelia immunomodulation meaningfully raise risk of other infections**
  (the immunosuppression that boosts *Babesia*)? [T8-S008]
