# Topic 8: Lyme, HIV, Syphilis, and WBC Disruption — Non-Consensus / Minority Research

> Flags: `mainstream` · `minority-research` · `fringe` · `historical` ·
> `speculative`. Inclusion is not endorsement.

## Overview

The non-consensus content centers on **chronic-Lyme immune-dysfunction
biomarkers** — most prominently the **CD57 natural-killer-cell test** — and the
broader minority claim that persistent *Borrelia* causes a chronic
immunodeficiency-like state that explains multi-system symptoms and opportunistic
co-infections. The underlying biology (Borrelia *does* suppress immunity
[T8-S002][T8-S003]) is mainstream; the contested part is the **specific clinical
biomarkers and the inference to a treatable chronic immunodeficiency**.

## The CD57 test (Stricker & Winger) — `minority-research`/`fringe`

Stricker and Winger reported that patients with "chronic Lyme disease" have
**decreased CD57+ natural-killer-cell subsets**, proposing CD57 as a biomarker of
chronic infection and treatment response [T8-S006]. In ILADS-aligned practice the
CD57 count is widely used to "stage" chronic Lyme and gauge antibiotic response.
Assessment:
- **Mainstream view:** CD57 as a chronic-Lyme biomarker is **not validated** —
  poor specificity, not reproduced in rigorous cohorts, not used in guideline
  diagnosis.
- **Honest status:** `minority-research` shading to `fringe` as a *clinical
  decision tool*. The general fact that *Borrelia* perturbs lymphocyte
  populations is real [T8-S001]; the leap to a specific, treatment-titrating NK
  marker is unsupported.

## "Chronic Lyme as acquired immunodeficiency" — `speculative`

A broader minority framing casts persistent *Borrelia* as producing an
HIV-adjacent chronic immune dysfunction, invoked to explain reactivated viruses
(EBV/CMV), recurrent infections, and co-infection stacking (Topic 5). The
mainstream germinal-center/humoral-suppression findings [T8-S002][T8-S003] give
this *partial* support — Borrelia genuinely impairs durable immunity — but the
sweeping "chronic immunodeficiency syndrome" claim is `speculative` and often
used to justify expansive empiric treatment.

## Cross-reactive serology as "immune confusion" — `minority-research`

Some minority writers fold the Lyme/syphilis/HIV serologic cross-reactivity
(Topic 7) into a narrative of generalized "immune confusion" caused by spirochetal
antigenic mimicry. The cross-reactivity is real [T7-S001][T7-S002]; the holistic
"immune-confusion" framing is interpretive overlay, not a defined mechanism.

## Where the minority is actually ahead of old consensus

It is worth noting honestly: the now-mainstream finding that **Borrelia broadly
suppresses humoral immunity and induces defective germinal centers**
[T8-S002][T8-S003] vindicates the *general intuition* (long held in patient/ILADS
circles) that Lyme "does something to the immune system." The minority was
directionally right that Borrelia is immunosuppressive; it overreached on the
*specific biomarkers and clinical claims*.

## Honest epistemic assessment

- **Borrelia suppresses humoral immunity / defective germinal centers** —
  `mainstream` [T8-S002][T8-S003].
- **Borrelia-driven B-cell lymphoma** — `mainstream` [T8-S004][T8-S005].
- **CD57 as a chronic-Lyme biomarker/treatment guide** — `minority-research`→
  `fringe`, unvalidated [T8-S006].
- **Chronic-Lyme acquired-immunodeficiency syndrome** — `speculative`.
