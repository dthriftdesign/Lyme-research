# Topic 8: Lyme, HIV, Syphilis, and WBC Disruption — Patient Reports

## Methodology and citation-integrity note

Patterns synthesized from r/Lyme, r/lymedisease, LymeNet, and immune/chronic-
illness communities (Phoenix Rising). Forum content is **paraphrased, never
quoted, never attributed to a user**; excerpts link to **platform-level URLs**
with **approximate eras**.

## Recurring Symptom Patterns

### Pattern: CD57 count as a tracked "Lyme severity" number
- **Description:** Members report following their **CD57 count** as a measure of
  chronic Lyme severity and treatment progress, sharing values and interpreting
  rises as improvement.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, LymeNet.
- **Frequency:** common in chronic-Lyme communities.
- **Relationship to literature:** reflects the CD57 minority biomarker [T8-S006];
  not validated by mainstream immunology [T8-S001].
- **Source IDs:** [T8-F001].

### Pattern: Frequent infections / reactivated viruses attributed to "Lyme immune suppression"
- **Description:** Reports of recurrent colds, shingles, EBV/CMV reactivation, and
  general "weak immune system" attributed to chronic Lyme.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, Phoenix Rising.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** partially consistent with documented Borrelia
  humoral suppression [T8-S002][T8-S003]; the holistic immunodeficiency framing is
  speculative.
- **Source IDs:** [T8-F002].

### Pattern: Abnormal CBC/lymphocyte findings shared and debated
- **Description:** Members post CBC results (low/high lymphocytes, atypical cells)
  and crowd-source interpretation, sometimes fearing lymphoma or HIV-like
  immunodeficiency.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme, r/lymedisease.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** Borrelia can cause real lymphoproliferation/
  lymphoma in rare cases [T8-S004][T8-S005]; most shared CBCs are nonspecific.
- **Source IDs:** [T8-F003].

### Pattern: Cross-reactive test anxiety (syphilis/HIV)
- **Description:** As in Topic 7, members describe alarm over cross-reactive
  positive treponemal or (rarely) HIV screening tests later attributed to
  tick-borne infection.
- **Platforms:** r/Lyme.
- **Frequency:** rare-to-occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** consistent with cross-reactivity literature
  [T7-S002][T8-S008-adjacent].
- **Source IDs:** [T8-F004].

## What patients describe that literature handles only partially

- The **lived sense of a "broken immune system"** is widespread and maps loosely
  onto real Borrelia humoral suppression — a place where patient intuition and
  emerging immunology partly converge, even as specific biomarkers (CD57) remain
  unvalidated.
