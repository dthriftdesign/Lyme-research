# Topic 3: Lyme, Morgellons, and Amphetamines — Patient Reports

## Methodology and citation-integrity note

Patterns synthesized from harm-reduction and stimulant communities
(PsychonautWiki, Bluelight.org, r/StopSpeeding, r/AdderallAddiction) alongside
Morgellons/Lyme communities (r/MorgellonsDisease, r/Lyme). This is a sensitive
crossover: substance-use disclosure is stigmatized in Lyme/Morgellons spaces and
infection beliefs are uncommon in stimulant spaces, so the two literatures rarely
mention each other — which is itself the key observation. Forum content is
**paraphrased, never quoted, never attributed to a user**; excerpts link to
**platform-level URLs** with **approximate eras**.

## Recurring Symptom Patterns

### Pattern: "Bugs under the skin" openly attributed to stimulants
- **Description:** In stimulant harm-reduction forums, users matter-of-factly
  describe formication ("shadow people," "meth mites," "coke bugs") during binges
  and compulsive picking, generally understanding it as a drug effect that fades
  with sleep/abstinence.
- **Platforms:** Bluelight, PsychonautWiki, r/StopSpeeding.
- **Frequency:** common (well-known effect in these communities).
- **Relationship to literature:** strongly confirms [T3-S001][T3-S002][T3-S003].
- **Source IDs:** [T3-F001].

### Pattern: Identical sensations attributed to infection in Lyme/Morgellons spaces
- **Description:** In Morgellons/Lyme communities, the same crawling/biting
  sensations and picking are attributed to *Borrelia*/parasites, with stimulant
  use rarely mentioned.
- **Platforms:** r/MorgellonsDisease, r/Lyme.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** the phenomenological identity supports the
  differential-diagnosis concern [T3-S002][T3-S004]; the attribution difference
  is the core cross-community contrast.
- **Source IDs:** [T3-F002].

### Pattern: Symptom resolution after stopping stimulants
- **Description:** Recovery-forum posters frequently report that formication and
  "infestation" feelings resolved within days to weeks of abstinence — sometimes
  to their own surprise.
- **Platforms:** r/StopSpeeding, r/AdderallAddiction, Bluelight.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** confirms the mainstream observation that
  stimulant DI remits with abstinence [T3-S001][T3-S004].
- **Source IDs:** [T3-F003].

### Pattern: Prescription-stimulant (ADHD) formication at higher doses
- **Description:** Some posters on prescribed amphetamines describe mild
  formication or skin-crawling at higher doses or with sleep deprivation,
  resolving with dose reduction.
- **Platforms:** r/AdderallAddiction, r/ADHD-adjacent harm-reduction threads.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** consistent with dose-dependent dopaminergic
  mechanism [T3-S003]; under-characterized in formal literature.
- **Source IDs:** [T3-F004].

## What patients describe that literature handles only partially

- **The disclosure gap itself:** the near-total absence of cross-reference
  between stimulant and infection communities for an identical symptom is a
  social pattern with direct diagnostic relevance — clinicians may never hear the
  drug history in a Lyme-framed visit.
- **Shame-driven non-disclosure** plausibly inflates the apparent number of
  "idiopathic" Morgellons cases.
