# Topic 2: Lyme and Morgellons — Patient Reports

## Methodology and citation-integrity note

Patterns synthesized from r/MorgellonsDisease, the Charles E. Holman Morgellons
Disease Foundation community (thecehf.org), r/Lyme, and legacy Morgellons
forums. Forum content is **paraphrased, never quoted, never attributed to a
named user**. As in Topic 1, excerpt records link to **platform-level URLs**
(not fabricated permalinks) and use **approximate eras**; this preserves
pattern-recognition value without inventing precision. This is an especially
sensitive community — many members feel dismissed by clinicians — and patterns
are reported descriptively, not diagnostically.

## Recurring Symptom Patterns

### Pattern: Fibers/filaments emerging from skin lesions
- **Description:** The defining report — colored (white/black/red/blue) fibers or
  filaments seen at or under non-healing lesions, often photographed under
  USB microscopes. Members treat the fibers' apparent emergence from skin as the
  core evidence of an organic disease.
- **Platforms:** r/MorgellonsDisease, CEHF community.
- **Frequency:** common (defining feature).
- **Relationship to literature:** The *existence* of collected fibers is
  acknowledged; origin is disputed — CDC/Hylwa find textile cellulose
  [T2-S001][T2-S003]; Middelveen reports endogenous keratin/collagen [T2-S006].
- **Source IDs:** [T2-F001].

### Pattern: Crawling/biting/stinging sensations (formication)
- **Description:** Persistent sensation of insects crawling or biting under the
  skin, often worse at night, driving scratching and lesion formation.
- **Platforms:** r/MorgellonsDisease, r/Lyme.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** Formication is real and documented; mainstream
  ties it to DI and/or stimulants [T2-S002] (and Topic 3); patients tie it to
  infection.
- **Source IDs:** [T2-F002].

### Pattern: Feeling labeled "delusional" and abandoning mainstream care
- **Description:** Strong, repeated accounts of clinicians (especially
  dermatologists) diagnosing delusional parasitosis, refusing tests, or
  prescribing antipsychotics; members describe this as traumatic and pivot to
  Lyme-literate / integrative practitioners.
- **Platforms:** r/MorgellonsDisease, CEHF.
- **Frequency:** common.
- **Relationship to literature:** Reflects the DI consensus framing [T2-S002]
  and the minority's "premature psychiatrization" critique [T2-S007].
- **Source IDs:** [T2-F003].

### Pattern: Co-existing or sought Lyme diagnosis
- **Description:** Many members report a positive Lyme test (often from specialty
  labs) or pursue one, and frame Morgellons as a skin manifestation of chronic
  Lyme. Tick-exposure histories are commonly cited.
- **Platforms:** r/MorgellonsDisease, r/Lyme, CEHF.
- **Frequency:** common within Morgellons communities.
- **Relationship to literature:** corroborates the demographic/symptom overlap
  reported by Savely & Stricker [T2-S008]; does not establish causation.
- **Source IDs:** [T2-F004].

### Pattern: Reported relief from antibiotics or antiparasitics
- **Description:** Some members report improvement of skin and systemic symptoms
  on long antibiotic or antiparasitic courses; others report no benefit or harm.
  Mixed, anecdotal, uncontrolled.
- **Platforms:** r/MorgellonsDisease, CEHF.
- **Frequency:** occasional.
- **Relationship to literature:** contradicts the mainstream position that
  antibiotics are not indicated [T2-S011]; consistent with the minority
  infectious model but confounded by placebo and natural fluctuation.
- **Source IDs:** [T2-F005].

## What patients describe that literature handles only partially

- **Lesion "granule/fiber expulsion" rituals** and elaborate self-documentation —
  a behavioral pattern more detailed in forums than literature, relevant to both
  the DI model (specimen sign) and the patient's lived experience.
- **Overlap with stimulant use** is rarely self-disclosed but occasionally
  surfaces (bridges to Topic 3).
