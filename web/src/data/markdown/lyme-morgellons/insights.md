# Topic 2: Lyme and Morgellons — Insights

> Emergent-pattern layer. Hypotheses labeled as such.

## Emergent Patterns

### Pattern: The whole dispute reduces to one unreplicated material-science fact
Strip away the politics and the disagreement is narrow and empirical: **are the
filaments cellulose (textile) or keratin/collagen (endogenous)?** The CDC/Hylwa
camp says textile [T2-S001][T2-S003]; the Middelveen camp says endogenous
[T2-S006]. This is resolvable by blinded spectroscopy with contamination
controls — a cheap experiment that, conspicuously, has not been done jointly.
The persistence of the controversy despite its tractability is itself a finding:
both sides are incentivized against a definitive neutral test (one risks losing
a disease, the other risks losing a diagnosis of exclusion).

### Pattern: "Real symptom, disputed cause" is the actual shape of the disease
Every camp agrees formication and lesions are real [T2-S002]. The fight is
purely causal. Framing Morgellons as "real vs. fake" misdescribes it; the honest
framing is **organic dermatosis vs. delusional infestation vs. a heterogeneous
mix of both** — and the recent mainstream review's call for standardized
criteria concedes the mix [T2-S011].

### Pattern: The Lyme link is sociological as much as biological
Morgellons communities and chronic-Lyme communities overlap heavily in
membership, clinicians, and explanatory frameworks [T2-F004][T2-S008]. Some of
the Lyme–Morgellons association is real comorbidity; some is **referral and
identity sorting** — patients dismissed as delusional migrate to Lyme-literate
practitioners who supply both an infection diagnosis and a community. Both
mechanisms can be true simultaneously.

## Hypotheses Worth Investigating
- **H1 (grounded, testable):** A blinded FTIR/Raman study of Morgellons filaments
  vs. textile controls would likely partition patients into a textile-fiber
  (DI-consistent) majority and a possible endogenous-filament minority. No
  published study has done this neutrally; it is the decisive experiment.
- **H2 (speculative):** If a true borrelial-dermatitis subgroup exists, the
  bovine digital dermatitis analogy [T2-S005] predicts it should show
  keratinocyte/fibroblast hyperproliferation co-localized with spirochetes on
  blinded histology — a specific, falsifiable prediction.
- **H3 (speculative):** A fraction of "Morgellons + Lyme" patients are
  stimulant-associated formication cases (Topic 3) whose drug use is undisclosed
  in Lyme-community settings; cross-screening would quantify this.

## Contradictions and Tensions
- **Cellulose [T2-S001][T2-S003] vs. keratin/collagen [T2-S006]** for the same
  filaments — the irreducible core contradiction.
- **Independent large study (CDC)** [T2-S001] **vs. single-group replication**
  [T2-S004][T2-S005][T2-S006] — an asymmetry of evidential weight the minority
  contests on grounds of bias and funding.
- **Patient-reported antibiotic relief** [T2-F005] **vs. absence of controlled
  evidence** [T2-S011].
